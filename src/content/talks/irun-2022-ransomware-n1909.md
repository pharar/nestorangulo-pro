"Horacio, what are you doing here? The police are handling this case." "I'm not sure — someone hired me anonymously to solve it. Can I help?" Let's dig into this intriguing case of ransomware in WordPress and how it was solved. We'll uncover some interesting clues, learn a few tricks, and weigh the countermeasures to avoid this situation in the future.
