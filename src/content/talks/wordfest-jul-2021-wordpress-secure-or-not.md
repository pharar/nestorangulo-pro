Is WordPress secure? The most-asked question in the community — and one of the most misunderstood. Rather than a yes/no, this talk unpacks what "secure" really means in the context of a CMS used by 40% of the web: who is responsible, what the realistic threats are, and what you can actually do about them. By the end, you'll have a clear framework for thinking about WordPress security — and a straight answer to take home.
