A hacked WordPress site does not get infected out of thin air. There must be an existing vulnerability that can be exploited, just as a ship does not sink with no reason.

This talk is intended to be a deep dive into the complexities of WordPress security, using real-life examples to illustrate the lifecycle of vulnerabilities and what we can do to prevent them. Our goal is to avoid falling into a ‘Bermuda Triangle’ of security threats, ensuring that our digital ‘ship’ continues its journey safely.
